# MyFantasyIQ Product Vision

## One-sentence vision

MyFantasyIQ is a paid fantasy football decision engine that tells ESPN fantasy managers their smartest next move.

## Core promise

A user should open the dashboard and immediately understand:
- how strong their team is
- what their biggest weakness is
- what move they should make next
- why that move makes sense
- how confident FantasyIQ is in the recommendation

## Target user

Serious fantasy football managers who want an edge but do not want to manually analyze every ranking, schedule, trade, waiver, and roster signal.

## Product personality

FantasyIQ should feel like a personal fantasy GM coach:
- direct
- sharp
- practical
- league-aware
- not gimmicky
- not overwhelming

## Product hierarchy

1. Main Move
2. FantasyIQ Score
3. Supporting Reasons
4. Risk Warning
5. Alternative Path
6. Feature tools that prove or execute the recommendation

## Core features

- Command Center
- FantasyIQ Score
- Roster IQ
- Draft Prep IQ
- Mock Simulator
- Big Board
- Trade IQ
- Waiver IQ
- Schedule IQ
- League Health
- Account/Admin/Payments

## Non-goals

- Do not become a generic fantasy news site.
- Do not depend on unreliable ESPN live draft sync as a core promise.
- Do not add features that do not improve the next-best-move recommendation.
- Do not overwhelm users with raw data.
- Do not hide uncertainty when data is incomplete.

## North-star question

Every feature must help answer:

What should I do next to improve my fantasy team?
