# MyFantasyIQ Brand Brief

Source: `docs/BRAND_SCRAPE.md` and `docs/firecrawl-myfantasyiq-home.json`

## Core Identity

- Brand name: MyFantasyIQ
- Product role: ESPN fantasy football command center
- Audience: Fantasy sports managers who want roster, waiver, trade, schedule, draft prep, and mock draft decisions in one place
- Tone: Professional, focused, medium-energy

## CTA Pattern

- Primary CTA: `Start Season Pass`
- Primary button: gold background, dark text, 8px radius
- Secondary CTA: `Check ESPN first`
- Secondary button: field-green background, cream text, 8px radius

## Premium Redesign Direction

Keep the brand rooted in sharp fantasy-football utility: dark field green, gold action color, compact executive-dashboard spacing, and clear command-center hierarchy. The site should feel like a paid decision engine, not a generic fantasy blog or a loud sports landing page.

Use the scrape as the brand source of truth, then push the interface toward: clearer first-screen value, stronger product screenshots, tighter pricing flow, richer trust/proof blocks, and more polished dashboard component states.
