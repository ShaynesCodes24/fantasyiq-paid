Privacy Policy

MyFantasyIQ collects only the information needed to configure and support a customer dashboard.

  • Customer setup information may include name, email, ESPN league ID, team ID, season, league name, league settings, draft state, and support notes.
  • Payment information is handled by Stripe. MyFantasyIQ does not store card numbers.
  • ESPN league data is fetched from ESPN public league endpoints when live sync is enabled.
  • We use account cookies for secure sign-in and may store operational logs for setup, login, sync, email, and payment troubleshooting.
  • We keep setup and support data while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for billing, fraud prevention, tax, legal, and support records.
  • You can request deletion or correction by emailing support@myfantasyiq.com. Some billing records may need to be retained where required.
  • Support emails may be handled by an email provider. Payment status and invoice details may be referenced through Stripe.
  • If we identify a security incident affecting customer data, we will use the checkout or account email to provide relevant notice.
  • Support requests should be sent to support@myfantasyiq.com.
