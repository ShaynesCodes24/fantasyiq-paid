# Customer Emails

Replace bracketed placeholders before sending.

## After Payment / Setup Email

```text
Subject: Your FantasyIQ dashboard is ready

Thanks for grabbing FantasyIQ.

Your dashboard is ready:
[dashboard link]

Access code:
[access code]

Next step:
Open the setup page and validate each public ESPN league you want connected:
[setup link]

Your Season Pass includes up to three public ESPN leagues. If you have more
than three leagues, use the add-league button in the dashboard for the $5/year
league add-on.

How to find the ESPN IDs:
1. Open your ESPN fantasy league in a web browser, not just the mobile app.
2. The league ID is the number after leagueId= in the URL.
3. Open your team page or roster page.
4. The team ID is the number after teamId= in the URL.
5. If you cannot find teamId=, send the full ESPN league URL and your team name.

Need help?
[help link]

Live draft sync currently requires a public ESPN league.

Support email:
support@myfantasyiq.com
```

## Public League Problem

```text
Subject: FantasyIQ live sync needs public ESPN access

I checked your ESPN league and the live sync endpoint is not publicly available.

FantasyIQ v1 supports public ESPN leagues. If you want live draft sync, please
make the league public before draft day and send me the league ID again.

The rest of the dashboard can still be configured, but live ESPN draft sync will
not work until the league is public.
```
